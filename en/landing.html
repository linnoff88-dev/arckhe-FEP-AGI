The thesis

Before, you prompted the task. Now you guide the agent. The prompt isn’t dead: it’s no longer the whole job; it’s become the brief.

What you’ll know how to do

Write an intention brief in four blocks (intent, boundaries, done when, record) for a real responsibility, without any prescribed steps.

Audit an old prompt by subtraction, removing the crutches that are getting in the way with the September 2026 models, and explain the shift in three sentences without exaggerating.

Who it’s for

Made for where you are now

is for you to

  • You own a small business, are self-employed, or manage a department, like the real estate agent, language school owner, clinic coordinator, and travel agency owner featured in the lessons.
  • You already use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini at work and feel that a “good prompt” is no longer enough.
  • You took FEP, FEP2, or another prompt course and want to know what comes next without starting from scratch.
  • You prefer to learn by doing, in 20- to 28-minute sessions, at your own pace.

isn't for you to
